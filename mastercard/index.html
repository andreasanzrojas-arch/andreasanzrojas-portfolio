Decision spine

Each decision removes a drop-off.

01 · POS

Guided selection + Recomendada

Profile-mapped recommendation while still showing all three models. The system should have a recommendation—not just options—so wrong-fit returns fall. Strongest local UI proof for this case.

02 · Security

Biometric at onboarding

Face ID / fingerprint as a required onboarding step—not optional settings later. Built in during the first session means it is never deferred and forgotten.

03 · Fiscal

Map-based address validation

Case evidence: replace street-level text with pin confirm/adjust; compliance data captured identically. Dedicated map UI frame is not in the local asset cache (filename d01 is splash/entry). Decision told in type until the map screen is recovered.

04–06 · Continuity layers

Help · Loyalty · Marketplace

Contextual multi-channel support at high-friction steps. Loyalty Bronce→Plata→Oro makes completion feel like a beginning. Marketplace embedded after POS—discovery at high intent. Local frames for help/loyalty/marketplace are missing; held as typography until recovered. Production crop marketplace.jpg is portfolio art only—not used as decision proof.

Architecture

One guided session

The design was not a series of screens. It was the architecture of a single guided onboarding that routes a merchant from first contact to first activated service without visiting a branch. Session entry splash (local d01), terminal, biometric, and dashboard form the available product material for that claim.

Impact

Soft outcomes. NDA-safe precision.

Activation moved from a multi-day branch process to a self-service mobile flow—days to minutes in operational terms, without invented percentages.

  • Marketplace adoption increased—merchants reached digital storefront setup through the same journey (no %)
  • Biometric configured inline at onboarding—no separate session
  • Customer service surfaced contextually at high-friction steps
  • Scalable platform architecture for future payment product rollouts
