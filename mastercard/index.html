Merchant activation

Unify the jobs without making the merchant leave the business.

Apart

POS fit, fiscal address, marketplace, and loyalty lived on separate timelines. Acquisition failed because the work was fragmented.

Days to minutes in one session. No invented percentage. Client confidential.

Framing

Unify the jobs without making the merchant leave the business.

POS acquisition still ran through branch and paper. Fiscal address was error-prone. Marketplace and loyalty lived elsewhere. Research mapped two personas — Sienna, who needed speed, and Mateo, who carried compliance — and a journey with five drop-off points: discovery, documents, fiscal, terminal selection, and abandonment after activation. A workshop moved 15 ideas to 5 prototypes.

  • POS fitThree models as equivalent specs produced wrong-fit returns. Guided selection with Recomendada is the strongest local UI proof.
  • Fiscal addressHighest drop-off. Street text mismatched how owners think about a place. Map confirm-or-adjust was the decision. The map frame is not in the local cache.
  • MarketplaceMerchants were paying third parties for a channel the bank already offered. Embedded after POS, at high intent. Not shown as a dedicated frame.
  • Loyalty and securityRewards were invisible. Biometrics had been deferred to settings and never finished.

Ownership

Full UX process, end to end. Co-facilitated the workshop with a dedicated facilitator. The architecture of a single guided onboarding was agreed with product, compliance, and engineering before screens.

Strip any one of these decisions and a drop-off returns. That is the continuity argument — not a row of features.

Impact, kept soft

Activation moved from a multi-day branch process to a self-service mobile flow. Days to minutes in operational terms. Marketplace reached through the same journey. Biometrics configured inline. Support surfaced at high-friction steps. No percentages. Quantitative detail stays under confidentiality. Monoma is not part of this journey.
