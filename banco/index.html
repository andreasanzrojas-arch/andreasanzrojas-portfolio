Research, kept specific

40 qualitative interviews and card-sorting, segmented by risk profile, financial literacy, and prior CDT experience. Three personas. The repeated finding: anxiety about locking savings without understanding outcomes blocked commitment more than product complexity. Most people discovered auto-renewal after maturity. Many had relied on a branch advisor, so digital had to provide the guidance.

End-to-end ownership: research synthesis, UX architecture, prototyping, visual UI, and post-launch Hotjar and funnel reading. Bank design system and brand were given. Risk and compliance co-designed every trust barrier.
