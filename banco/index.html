Banco de Bogotá CDT

Twelve analog steps became simulate, validate, confirm.

Evidence

After the renewal hold: +30% overall CDT openings after launch — not digital-only. Shared with product and go-to-market.

Twelve analog steps became simulate, validate, confirm. Fully digital — no branch visit, no physical documents. Auto-renewal complaints decreased, with no invented percentage, and renewal stays manageable in the app.

40 interviews and card-sorting, three personas. Anxiety about locking savings without understanding the outcome blocked commitment more than product complexity. Most people found auto-renewal only after maturity. The toggle was contested across Product, Compliance, and business, then approved with continued control in the app. Simulation had the highest engagement and the lowest drop-off — Hotjar and funnels, directional, no percentage invented. Ownership ran from research synthesis through post-launch reading. The bank’s design system was given.
