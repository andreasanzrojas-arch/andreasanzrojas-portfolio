**Status:** ACTIVE — autonomous build after Homepage FREEZE (Sep 18, 2026)

## 1. Purpose

One **shared case-study system** so the three journeys feel like the same mind and craft — without three identical pages or three unrelated art directions.

Each case **inherits the system**, then **breaks it** only where product evidence or narrative behavior demands it (Phase 3/4).

Visitor learning goals (from Phase 2, frozen):
- **Google:** reject cosmetic brief → multi-audience discovery system; ship under Google constraints; no fake outcome %.
- **Banco de Bogotá:** trust-before-compliance compression; 12→3; contested auto-renewal; **+30% overall CDT openings**.
- **Mastercard Merchant Activation:** fragmented merchant jobs → one guided session; soft/NDA-safe outcomes; Monoma **not** merged.

Evidence must surface: **framing, ownership, decisions, trade-offs, stakeholders, systems thinking, impact** — only when evidenced.

**Shared page skeleton (template — not a rigid section stack):**
1. Nav
2. Case entry (client · title · role/year · stakes)
3. Framing / problem pressure
4. Ownership + collaborators
5. Decision spine (varies spatially per case)
6. System / product material (behavior-specific)
7. Impact / honesty close
8. Reflection only if evidenced
9. Next case / contact bridge
10. Footer

**Forbidden:** Situation eyebrow stamp; identical left/right/product-below for all three; Field Gravity / Decision Trace / OPEN FOR ROLES; Version B dark+giant-serif; invented KPIs/research.
