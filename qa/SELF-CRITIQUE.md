# Self-critique

Viewports checked: 1440×900, 1280×800, 1280×720, 390×844. Reduced motion at 1440.

## Laptop

1. **Who is this, before I do anything?** Andrea Sanz Rojas, Senior Product & Experience Designer, then four ledger lines: shipped product, UX + UI craft, AI-native, Europe. All sit above the stage edge at 1440, 1280×800, and 1280×720.
2. **Is one stage dominant?** The first view is type plus one product edge, not three screenshots. After the frame rises, one project owns the viewport.
3. **What stayed, what changed?** The frame’s edges and top rule stay. Arrival is masked as the frame grows. Google gives way to Banco by a horizontal clip. Banco gives way to Merchant by opening on the vertical center. Audience choice widens a column. Banco swaps the plate in the same frame. Mastercard moves the same devices along a thread.
4. **Is the interaction the argument?** Audience recomposition, the renewal gate for +30%, the playhead, and Lab Approve/Revise. None of those are decoration.
5. **Does it feel edited at this width?** The ledger is a tight baseline, the bar is one 48px row at 1280 and 1440, and the controls sit in the bar instead of on the UI. The labels beat is a short filter asset centered in the frame — the file is 210px tall, so the field around it stays empty on purpose.

## Mobile

1. **Who is this?** The same five facts are in the first screen. The stage starts on the next screen.
2. **One stage?** One project, switched with Google / Banco / Merchant or a horizontal swipe.
3. **Continuity?** No tween. State changes are instant, which is the reduced-motion and narrow rule.
4. **Interaction?** The same controls, with 44px targets, in the bar rather than over the screen.
5. **Edited?** The name is the large mark. The ledger stacks and sits low, with air between the role and the facts. The Europe line stays inside the first viewport.

## If the project content were removed, would this be a premium template?

The type-and-ledger opening is in the family of editorial portfolio templates. What is not interchangeable is the stage: one frame that masks the arrival, then wipes and re-axes between these three products, with an audience column that actually recomposes and a metric that stays hidden until renewal. That behavior is the work, not a theme. No extra effect was added to pass this test.

## Honest limits

- `google-labels.png` is a 1440×210 filter bar. It cannot fill the stage without being enlarged into blur.
- The portrait file is already a square medium shot. The page shows the whole file, small, with no tighter face crop. It cannot reveal a full-body frame the source does not contain.
- Desktop project changes are scroll-scrubbed clips. Very fast flicks can cross a boundary before the eye settles; the in-bar controls jump back.
- Reduced motion and narrow widths do not play the rising-frame gesture. They show the arrival, then one project at a time.
