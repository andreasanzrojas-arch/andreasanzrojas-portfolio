## Would a Design Director prefer V2?

Yes, for the work. Not because it moves more. Because the first screen shows who Andrea is and one real product at a readable scale, and the case states the decision before the prose.

## Verdict by criterion

| Criterion | Who wins | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immediate comprehension | V2 | Name, role, three project lines, the four facts, and a large Google UI are all in the first viewport. LIVE leads with a headline and two small thumbnails. |
| Visual hierarchy | V2 | One product owns the right side. LIVE gives the headline and the thumbnails similar weight. |
| Project storytelling | V2 on the case, LIVE on the home headline | V2 Google opens with “Better sorting would not unlock discovery.” over the hub, and the audience control is the story. LIVE’s case headline is clear, then a paragraph. V2 Banco opens with “Twelve analog steps became simulate, validate, confirm.” |
| UX / navigation | Tie, slight V2 | Both can reach Google, Banco, and contact. V2’s project index and case link sit on the work. LIVE’s Work / About / Contact labels are more conventional and still clear. |
| UI craft | V2 | The product is the material. LIVE’s cards and dark case template are polished and familiar. |
| Perceived seniority | V2, with one LIVE advantage | Seeing the shipped UI at scale reads more senior than a thumbnail. LIVE’s line “Leading complex product design at global scale.” is the stronger single claim. |
| Interaction design | V2 | Audience columns, the Banco sequence, the Mastercard thread, and Lab Approve / Revise are the product. LIVE’s case is read-only. |
| Memorability | V2 | The split — name on the left, one product on the right, then that frame taking the screen — is specific to this work. |

## Where LIVE still wins

- Contact utility. LIVE has a message form, email, LinkedIn, and a CV. V2 has email and LinkedIn, a small full-frame portrait, and no form. The resume PDF is not in this branch, so it is not linked.
- The positioning sentence. LIVE says the seniority claim in one line. V2 uses the role plus the work, and does not import that headline.
- LIVE has no Lab, so there is nothing to beat there. V2’s lab is the AI proof LIVE only describes.
